Contact & Support

Have a question about our software or a product in development? Visitors can contact Coleto Creek Software Solutions using the email address below.

Email us

Our public support and contact address is:

support@coletocreeksoftwaresolutions.com

Use this address for product questions or to describe a problem. You can also copy the address into your email app.

Do not email sensitive information

Do not send any of the following:

  • Passwords
  • Social Security numbers
  • Bank account numbers
  • Credit or debit card numbers
  • Full tax identification numbers
  • Financial account login information

Before sending a screenshot, check it for sensitive or personal information. Remove or cover those details before attaching it. Check your message and any other attachments as well.

When contacting us

A few clear, non-sensitive details help us understand your question and diagnose a problem. If you are reporting an issue, include:

  • The product name.
  • Your Windows version, if relevant and you know it.
  • What you were trying to do.
  • What happened instead.
  • The exact error message, if one appeared, with any sensitive details removed.
  • A screenshot, if useful, after checking it for sensitive or personal information.

You do not need technical language. Describe the steps you took in your own words and explain where you got stuck. If we need more details to understand the issue, we may ask follow-up questions.
